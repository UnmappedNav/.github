# Claude instructions

Follow repository instructions and conventions.

## No AI co-authorship

AI models, assistants, and coding agents (including Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, and GitHub Copilot) must **never be credited as an author or co-author** of any Unmapped work. Do not add AI attribution or `Co-authored-by:` trailers to Git commits, or list AI as a co-author in pull requests, issues, source files, documentation, releases, or other project artifacts. Remove automatically generated AI co-author credits before committing or publishing.

AI tools may assist with work, but authorship and responsibility belong solely to the human contributors who review and accept that work.
