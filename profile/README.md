## About Unmapped

Unmapped Navigation LLC is developing a privacy-conscious navigation platform designed around outdoor exploration, road trips, overlanding, camping, and travel beyond traditional destinations.

Many navigation tools work well when the destination has an address and a reliable cellular connection. Outdoor travel often looks different.

Unmapped is being built around that reality.

Our focus includes:

- Reliable navigation for travel on and off the beaten path
- Strong offline functionality
- Trip planning for outdoor adventures
- Useful geographic and trail information
- Clear, purpose-built navigation experiences
- Privacy-conscious product design
- Tools designed for real-world conditions

We believe navigation software should help people explore without becoming a distraction from the experience itself.

## Our Principles

### Offline matters

Connectivity should not determine whether your navigation tools remain useful. Unmapped is designed with offline use as a fundamental consideration rather than an afterthought.

### Privacy by design

Location information can be extremely sensitive. We aim to collect only what is necessary to provide our services and to design systems that minimize unnecessary collection and retention of personal information.

### Useful over complicated

More information does not always create a better navigation experience. We prioritize information that helps users understand where they are, where they are going, and what they need to know along the way.

### Built for exploration

Unmapped is intended for people heading somewhere interesting — whether that means a campsite, trail, forest road, scenic drive, or somewhere that doesn't fit neatly into a street address.

### Safety matters

Navigation software can influence real-world decisions. We take reliability, clarity, and responsible presentation of information seriously.

## Security

We take security issues seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability involving an Unmapped product or service, please **do not disclose it publicly through a GitHub issue**.

Report security concerns privately to:

**security@unmappednav.com**

Please include enough information for us to understand and reproduce the issue. Avoid accessing, modifying, or retaining user information beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability.
