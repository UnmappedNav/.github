# Unmapped Navigation

### Explore Beyond.

**Navigation built for the journey beyond the pavement.**

Unmapped Navigation is developing an offline-first navigation and trip-planning experience for people who take the scenic route, explore public lands, find campsites, and travel beyond reliable cellular coverage.

Whether the destination is a trailhead, a forest road, a campground, or a stop along a cross-country drive, our goal is to make the journey easier to plan and navigate—without turning exploration into a complicated process.

## What we're building

We want one place to plan a trip, understand the route and its surroundings, and keep navigating when connectivity becomes unreliable.

Our product direction includes:

- **Offline-first navigation:** Downloadable map coverage and navigation resources designed to remain useful away from service.
- **Thoughtful trip planning:** Multi-stop routes, useful stops along the way, and tools for road trips, camping, and outdoor travel.
- **Scenic exploration:** Routing and discovery tools that help travelers enjoy the journey, not just reach an address.
- **Outdoor map context:** Relevant information about trails, trailheads, campsites, public lands, and roads where data is available.
- **Practical travel awareness:** Planned access to contextual information such as weather alerts, wildfires, road or trail restrictions, and offline-coverage considerations.
- **A focused driving experience:** An iPhone-first experience with CarPlay-related functionality in development, designed to keep essential navigation information clear and accessible.

Data coverage, freshness, and availability may vary by region and source. Navigation and hazard information should always be checked against current official guidance and on-the-ground conditions.

## Why Unmapped?

Conventional navigation is excellent at getting people from one street address to another. Exploring outdoors often requires more: limited signal, destinations without conventional addresses, changing conditions, and roads or trails that need additional context.

We believe those needs deserve a navigation experience designed around them from the beginning.

Our guiding principles:

1. **Offline is foundational.** Useful navigation should not depend entirely on a live data connection.
2. **Clarity over clutter.** Show the information that matters, when it matters.
3. **Privacy is important.** Treat location and trip information as sensitive and minimize unnecessary collection and retention.
4. **Reliability earns trust.** Prefer clear limitations and data provenance over false precision.
5. **Exploration should feel like exploration.** Technology should support the experience, not dominate it.

## How we build

Unmapped's engineering work spans mobile navigation, geospatial data processing, backend services, and operational tooling. Current development areas include:

- iOS app development and in-car navigation experiences
- Map rendering, routing, and large-scale trail and road geometry
- Offline data packaging, compression, and regional distribution
- Geospatial datasets for outdoor recreation and public lands
- Data ingestion, deduplication, publication, and quality checks
- Service availability, monitoring, and privacy-conscious diagnostics

This is a description of our engineering focus, not a public API or compatibility commitment. The implementation and product roadmap may change as we test.

## Security and responsible disclosure

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in an Unmapped product or service, please report it **privately** to [security@unmappednav.com](mailto:security@unmappednav.com), rather than opening a public GitHub issue.

Include a concise description, affected component, reproduction steps, and potential impact where possible. Do not access, change, download, or retain anyone else's data to demonstrate a problem.
